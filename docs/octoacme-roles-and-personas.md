# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Facilitators

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Facilitators improve team flow by facilitating ceremonies, coaching the team on process, and removing delivery impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies, including planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach the team on lightweight, iterative delivery practices
- Identify and remove blockers to team collaboration and delivery
- Make workflow issues, impediments, and delivery trends visible
- Protect collaboration time and encourage continuous improvement

### Goals
- Improve predictability and reduce avoidable delivery friction
- Create a healthy, collaborative team environment
- Shorten cycle time and improve flow

### Decision Scope
- Facilitates team process decisions and recommends changes to ways of working
- Escalates cross-team, resource, or governance decisions to the Project Manager and delivery leadership
- Does not set product priorities or unilaterally change agreed scope

### Interactions and Handoffs
- Partner with Project Managers on schedules, dependencies, resource constraints, and delivery risks
- Partner with Product Managers to improve backlog readiness and clarify priorities
- Work with Developers to expose blockers, improve workflow, and support sustainable delivery
- Raise unresolved impediments or cross-project dependencies through the Project Manager's established escalation process

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers turn customer needs into usable, accessible experiences and communicate design intent to delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize customer and usability research
- Define interaction patterns and usability standards
- Create prototypes, workflows, and interface designs
- Validate accessibility and inclusive design requirements
- Communicate design rationale and work with implementation teams

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer outcomes
- Deliver consistent, accessible experiences
- Resolve customer and technical tradeoffs early

### Decision Scope
- Owns design exploration, usability recommendations, and interaction decisions within approved product direction
- Recommends changes that affect scope or product outcomes to the Product Manager
- Escalates feasibility or implementation constraints through the Product Manager and Project Manager

### Interactions and Handoffs
- Work with Product Managers to translate customer and business needs into design requirements and success criteria
- Partner with Developers on feasible implementation, design reviews, and technical constraints
- Provide acceptance-ready designs and clarify outstanding questions before work enters implementation
- Raise unresolved customer, scope, or delivery tradeoffs to the Product Manager and Project Manager

---

## QA Leads / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Leads establish quality ownership, test strategy, and evidence-based readiness for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and ownership across functional, integration, accessibility, performance, and regression testing
- Define test entry and exit criteria and quality metrics
- Coordinate defect triage, investigation, and resolution
- Identify product and technical risks that require additional validation
- Maintain test plans and verify that acceptance criteria can be tested

### Goals
- Prevent defects and reduce release risk
- Make quality status and residual risk visible
- Establish consistent, measurable validation practices

### Decision Scope
- Defines test approaches and recommends release-risk mitigations
- Recommends changes to acceptance criteria or test coverage to Product Managers
- Escalates unresolved defects or test readiness concerns through the Project Manager's risk process

### Interactions and Handoffs
- Collaborate with Developers on quality ownership, testability, defect reproduction, and resolution
- Collaborate with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and product risk
- Work with Project Managers to report test readiness, schedule risk, and critical defects
- Coordinate with Release Managers or DevOps Leads on release validation, smoke testing, and rollback verification

---

## Release Managers / DevOps Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Leads coordinate safe, repeatable delivery of approved work from environment readiness through post-deployment verification.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness and deployment planning
- Maintain awareness of environment and pipeline availability
- Document and execute deployment and rollback procedures
- Secure operational approvals and confirm post-deployment verification
- Communicate operational risk and release status

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, repeatable releases
- Minimize deployment disruption and recovery time
- Provide clear go/no-go evidence and operational ownership

### Decision Scope
- Owns operational release execution within approved scope and governance
- Recommends go/no-go decisions based on technical readiness and operational risk
- Escalates unresolved release, environment, or rollback concerns through the established risk and sponsorship process

### Interactions and Handoffs
- Plan deployment activities with Project Managers and Developers
- Confirm release scope and customer value with Product Managers
- Coordinate test evidence and release criteria with QA Leads or Test Leads
- Escalate operational risks, dependency delays, or no-go recommendations through the Project Manager and relevant stakeholders

---

## Stakeholders / Business Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Sponsors provide executive context, strategic direction, funding support, and organizational decision-making for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic context and clarify business priorities
- Approve or champion scope, funding, and major tradeoffs
- Remove organizational barriers and help resolve cross-team issues
- Participate in major decisions and status reviews
- Communicate business impacts and sponsor-level expectations

### Goals
- Ensure delivery remains aligned with strategic outcomes
- Resolve high-impact organizational risks and tradeoffs
- Provide clear sponsorship and organizational support

### Decision Scope
- Approves major scope, funding, strategic priority, and sponsor-level risk decisions
- Does not replace the Product Manager's day-to-day prioritization or the Project Manager's delivery coordination
- High-impact sponsor decisions are recorded and communicated through the project's normal governance process

### Interactions and Handoffs
- Set strategic direction and success expectations with Product Managers
- Receive coordinated plans, status, risks, and decisions from Project Managers
- Resolve executive tradeoffs with the Product Manager, Project Manager, and delivery team
- Help unblock organizational dependencies and sponsor major risk or release decisions
